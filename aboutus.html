About Us

Learn more about our barangay and its community.

Our Vision

Barangay Pugay is a primary rice producer barangay in San Jose with self-reliant and God-fearing residents, ecologically-balanced environment, with infrastructures essential to human existence and demonstrating competence, compassion and honesty in local public service.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through the practice of honesty and dedication among its employees, officials, by keeping the communal and cultural ties, and enhancing personal and professional growth, and by creating an effective and collaborative teamwork projects for the welfare and development of the community.
